About

Beyond the court.

COURTSCAPE discovers extraordinary places around the world where tennis meets travel.

We believe the best tennis trips aren’t only about the match. They’re about the court at the edge of a cliff, the coach who knows the wind, the long lunch after.

COURTSCAPE is an independent tennis travel publication and destination guide. We seek out beautiful courts — at resorts, hotels, clubs and hidden corners — and tell you honestly what it’s like to play, stay and explore there.

Our first editorial focus is Indonesia, beginning with Bali and Lombok. From there we travel outward: the lagoons of the Maldives, the summer clay of the Swiss Alps, the highland and island courts of Japan, and beyond.

Our standards

  • We label how we know. Every listing shows whether it was personally reviewed, verified with the venue, sourced from public information, community submitted, or not yet verified — with dates.
  • We never invent facts. If we don’t know a court’s surface, price or access rules, we say so.
  • Scores are earned. The Tennis Escape Score is only published after we’ve played there.
  • Independence. Partnerships will always be disclosed and never buy a score.

Work with us

We partner with resorts, clubs and tourism boards who share our love of the game and the place. Get in touch.
