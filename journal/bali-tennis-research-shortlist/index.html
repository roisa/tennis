Bali tennis: our research shortlist

From the Bukit clifftops to the caldera highlands — the Bali courts we are investigating first, and what we know so far.

By COURTSCAPE Editors · 3 November 2026 · 1 min read

In this story

  1. The south: cliffs and bays
  2. Ubud and the interior
  3. The highlands
  4. What happens next

Bali is where COURTSCAPE begins. The island packs remarkably different tennis settings into a short drive: limestone clifftops in the south, a long sandy bay at Jimbaran, the river valleys around Ubud and cool, often misty highlands in the north.

This is a research shortlist, not a ranking. Each venue below appears in public sources as offering tennis. None has yet been visited or rated by COURTSCAPE, and every listing shows its sources and what remains unconfirmed.

The south: cliffs and bays

On the Bukit Peninsula, The Ungasan Clifftop Resort describes a synthetic grass court. Around Jimbaran, Raffles Bali lists clay-surface courts for in-house guests, and the InterContinental Bali Resort lists a beachfront court.

Ubud and the interior

Amandari, on the edge of Ubud, describes a floodlit hard court with hitting partners. Elsewhere in the island's green interior, Kula Mani Tennis Village is built around tennis itself, with hard and red clay courts.

The highlands

In the caldera valley near Bedugul, Handara Golf & Resort describes an outdoor mountain court that public sources say non-guests can hire too.

What happens next

We are contacting each venue to confirm court counts, surfaces, access and booking. As we confirm details, labels change from Sourced from public information to Information verified with venue — and after a visit, to Personally reviewed, with a full Tennis Escape Score.
